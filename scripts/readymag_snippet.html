Speakers

Giovanna Crise

multidisciplinary designer & brand strategist at illo.tv

Giovanna is a multidisciplinary designer from Trieste, currently based in Torino. With a passion for storytelling, photography, and interactive design, her work explores the intersection of culture and creativity. One of her favorite personal projects made with Readymag, Korea Travel Zine, blends editorial and digital design through an interactive lens. She currently works as a brand strategist at illo.tv, where she oversees the studio’s image.

Lino Di Maio and Laura Sauchelli

Helena Sciavarrello

sainthelenastudio

Co-founders of Pop-Eye Studio

Helena is a designer, content creator, and design educator based in Torino. Drawing inspiration from her roots and culture, she brings authenticity to every project—from branding and illustration to social media and beyond. Passionate about meaningful (and fun) work, Helena shares design insights, tutorials, and behind-the-scenes glimpses of her creative process on TikTok, where she’s built a vibrant community of fellow designers.

Pop-Eye Studio is a Milan-based creative studio turning different ideas into bold visuals. They mix graphic design, illustration, digital products and a pinch of weirdness to help brands stand out. From logos to websites to visual stories that would make your eyeballs happy, they craft everything with love, color, and just the right amount of chaos. The name is a nod to Lino's sailor grandpa story—always watching, always exploring.

Esenia Hortus

Social media and community lead at Readymag

Esenia is a social media and community strategist based in Barcelona. At Readymag, she leads community initiatives and oversees social media and the ambassador program — connecting designers from around the world. Esenia will give a live demo at the meetup, sharing recent Readymag updates and practical tips.
